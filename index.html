How it works

From first call to kits on campus.

  1. Tell us what you teach

    Book a call or send a quote request: the programs, how many students start each term, and any branding you want.

  2. Get a quote the same day

    A line-by-line quote per kit, with your cost and a suggested student price, the same business day.

  3. Approve a sample

    For larger orders, a complete sample kit goes to your campus first. Swaps at this stage are free.

  4. We deliver and restock

    Kits arrive boxed per student for each intake. You get an invoice and pay online by bank transfer or card.
